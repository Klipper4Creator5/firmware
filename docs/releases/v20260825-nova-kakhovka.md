Releases are named for a Ukrainian city under occupation. This one is
**Nova Kakhovka**, taken by russian forces in 2022. For a couple of days
mechanised brigades came through the city and on towards the right bank of
the Dnipro, across the dam. The dam was destroyed later; the right bank was
freed. Nova Kakhovka is still occupied today.

*https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Not7jbKjybg*

---

This is the rebuilt v20260824-nova-kakhovka, keeping its name. The
v20260824 packages were built without the Klipper fork — on a printer
already running this mod they left a mixed klippy tree and Klipper failed
at startup with `Internal error during connect: ... expects 4 arguments,
got 3`. Those packages have been withdrawn; installing this build on top
repairs an affected printer.
